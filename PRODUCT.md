# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers who want to understand and use AI tutorials and practical notes. The owner explicitly chose this as the primary visitor goal.

## Product Purpose

superzyk.com is a personal homepage and blog. Help readers choose a relevant article, understand prerequisites, follow its steps, and find related reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Maintain the existing Next.js personal site, Markdown articles, URLs, and metadata.
- The homepage extension adds one owner-approved pixel-art campfire scene while preserving the existing reading structure. Repository uploads and production releases require the owner's explicit authorization.
- World Cup functionality is archived and outside scope.
- Publication, revision, and verification dates have distinct meanings. A visual update is not tutorial revalidation.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the personal site identity and cyan/teal accent. References supplied by the owner: mmguo.dev and 1991421.cn. The approved reading direction remains a short introduction, one list with each article appearing once, and a compact contact footer. One pixel-art scene accompanies the introduction: a large tree against a gradient starry sky, a boy at lower left, a campfire at lower center, and a cat and dog at lower right. Its lightweight motion and interaction must preserve a useful static view and accessible controls. Search and categories live in the archive; long articles keep the table of contents without a duplicate reading guide or progress display.

## Evidence on Hand

Five public Markdown articles: two MCP explainers and three Agent/local-model tutorials. GitHub and email are existing contact channels. The campfire artwork was generated for this site; its source prompt is retained in `artifacts/pixel-campfire/prompt.txt`, with the local animation build documented in that directory's `README.md`. No biography, project achievements, or fresh verification claims have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Help readers choose by their goal and environment.
- Prioritize readable content and working examples.
- Attribute content without inventing credentials.
- Preserve meaningful article navigation and accessible controls.
